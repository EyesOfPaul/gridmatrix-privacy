GridMatrix™ Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 26, 2026
Developer: Anton Paul Le Breton (EyesOfPaul) · Contact: aplz86nz@gmail.com

The short version

GridMatrix is an offline-first app. Everything you log stays on your device. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and the developer never receives your data.

What stays on your device

Everything you create in GridMatrix — items, photos, notes, reserves, medical records, fuel, bags and checklists, waypoints, solar panel pins, settings and saved map areas — is stored only in the app's storage on your device. Backup files and PDFs you save go to your device's Documents folder, and what you do with them is up to you.

Location

GridMatrix uses your device's GPS only if you allow it, using the 📍 Allow GPS Location switch and your device's permission prompt. It is used for:

  • The Solar Forecast. When you tap Sync, your coordinates (to four decimal places) are sent to the Open-Meteo forecast service to fetch the forecast for your location.
  • Your position on the Nav map. Your position is shown on the map and never leaves your device. The app remembers your last position on the device so the map opens where you were.

Waypoints and solar panel locations you save are stored on your device like the rest of your data. You can switch location off at any time; the rest of the app keeps working.

Internet connections

GridMatrix works without an internet connection. Its map display library is built into the app, and its text uses a font bundled with the app or your phone's own, so neither is downloaded. When you're online, it contacts only these services. Each one sees your device's internet (IP) address as part of the connection, but none of them receives your logged data:

  • Open-Meteo — the solar forecast, as described above. open-meteo.com/en/terms
  • OpenStreetMap — map tiles for the area you're viewing on the Nav map. Map areas you save offline are stored on your device. osmfoundation.org/wiki/Privacy_Policy
  • GitHub Pages — a small file checked at startup to see whether an update is available. GitHub General Privacy Statement
  • Google Play — only when you tap Check for Updates, which opens the app's Play Store listing.

AI scanning

GridMatrix never sends your data to an AI service. You copy the AI Scan Prompt into an AI app of your choice, and paste the answer back into GridMatrix yourself. What you share in that AI app is governed by that app's own privacy policy.

Sharing

Data only leaves GridMatrix when you choose to share it — for example, sharing a Status Report or copying a backup to your clipboard. It goes only to the app or place you choose.

Read Aloud and the tutorial

Spoken text is produced by your device's own text-to-speech service. Depending on your device's voice settings, that service may process speech online under its own policy.

Permissions

  • Location — the Solar Forecast and your position on the Nav map (optional).
  • Camera and photos — only when you choose to attach a photo to an item.
  • Motion sensors — the compass.
  • File storage — saving backups and PDFs to your Documents folder.

Deleting your data

Delete everything you've logged in ⚙️ Settings → ☢️ System Reset Options → Wipe All Logged Data, or by uninstalling the app. Backup files and PDFs you saved to your Documents folder stay there until you delete them. Because the developer never receives your data, there is nothing held elsewhere to delete.

Children

GridMatrix is not directed at children and is not intended for anyone under 18.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new effective date.
